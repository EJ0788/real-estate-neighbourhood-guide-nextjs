SD48 School Overview

Every Squamish School, Rated & Mapped

Six elementary schools, each serving a distinct set of neighbourhoods. Knowing which catchment you’re buying into is as important as knowing the price per square foot.

Garibaldi Highlands Elementary
Grades K–6 · Garibaldi Highlands, Garibaldi Estates. The top-rated school in Squamish — families pay a measurable premium to be in this catchment.
6.1/10 Fraser Rating
Squamish Elementary
Grades K–6 · Downtown, Sea + Sky, Oceanfront. Home to the only French Immersion program at the elementary level in the Howe Sound feeder area.
5.7/10 Fraser Rating · FI
Valleycliffe Elementary
Grades K–6 · Valleycliffe, Hospital Hill, Crumpit Woods, Dentville, Northyards. Strong community school at excellent value relative to GHE catchment prices.
5.5/10 Fraser Rating
Brackendale Elementary
Grades K–6 · Brackendale, Tantalus. Small, tight-knit school surrounded by the Squamish River estuary — reflects the village character of north Squamish.
5.2/10 Fraser Rating
Mamquam Elementary
Grades K–6 · Garibaldi Estates, University Heights. Solid community school — a value option for families who want the Garibaldi area without the Highlands premium.
5.2/10 Fraser Rating
Britannia Beach Elementary
Grades K–6 · Britannia Beach. One of BC’s smallest and most unique schools — multi-grade classrooms, intimate community, heritage coastal setting.
N/R Not Ranked
Don Ross Middle School · Grades 7–9
All Squamish neighbourhoods. French Immersion continues here from Squamish Elementary. Strong electives and sports programs.
All Areas District-Wide · FI
Howe Sound Secondary · Grades 10–12
All Squamish neighbourhoods. French Immersion graduation pathway, trades and career education, outdoor education — one of BC’s best.
All Areas District-Wide · FI
